Next Level Gaming & Novelties — Interactive Event Rentals in NH & MA

Next Level Gaming & Novelties provides turnkey event rentals in NH, MA, and across New England. Based in Hudson, New Hampshire and serving Nashua, Manchester, Concord, Greater Boston, Massachusetts, Connecticut, Rhode Island, Maine, and Vermont, our team delivers, sets up, and staffs interactive entertainment for colleges, universities, K-12 schools, corporate gatherings, community festivals, and private parties.

For over ten years, founders Calvin Reid and Yaw Boateng have produced high-energy campus and community experiences that bring crowds together. Every package includes commercial-grade equipment, giant high-definition screens, concert audio, and dedicated on-site attendants so hosts can relax and enjoy the celebration with their guests.
